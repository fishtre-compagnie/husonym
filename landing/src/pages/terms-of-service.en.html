When a subscription ends

Husonym does not stop abruptly. It moves through four stages, each signalled in the interface.

StageWhenEffect
Activemore than 30 days remainingeverything works
Expiringwithin 30 days of expiryeverything works, a banner shows the date
Grace periodafter expiry, for a further periodeverything still works
Expiredafter the grace periodnew runs stop

Nothing is deleted. Once the grace period ends, creating and running jobs stops, but you keep access to all of your configuration and run history. You can still pause, cancel a run in progress and delete resources. Runs already under way are allowed to finish. Installing a renewed key restores full operation immediately, with no migration and no reconfiguration.
